# 0006: M4b solo mode

Date: 2026-09-26. Status: accepted, pending the device check.

## Context

Solo mode (spec 4.3) completes M4: the game hides the creatures itself around the player, they may move during the round, the rare species appears under its condition and hints sound from where the creature is.

## Decisions

- **Room scan**: "Enséñame la habitación" runs the spot finder twice a second on the latest depth map. Spots are stored as world directions, together with the direction of the nearer object next to each one, the light around it and the phone's up direction. Spots within 6° are the same spot seen twice; the better one is kept.
- **Coverage** is counted in eight compass directions from where the camera looks (ignored when the phone points nearly straight up or down). The game hides the creatures once half the room has been seen and there are enough spots for the difficulty. After a full turn it plays with whatever it found, down to two spots, so a bare room is not a dead end. Without a gyroscope (desktop), directions cannot be counted and any two spots will do.
- **Difficulty** is one preset (design brief): Fácil 3 creatures that peek out and stay put; Normal 5, a little, moving every 30–40 s; Difícil 7, barely peeking, moving every 20–30 s. How much a creature peeks is where it sits between the spot and the nearer object: half-way back out, on the spot, or half-way behind.
- **Placement** picks spots one direction after another in random order, best first, at least 15° apart, so creatures end up all around the player. A three-second countdown covers the camera while they appear.
- **Moving creatures** (when the parent area allows it): a creature out of view moves to a free spot the scan found, also out of view, so nobody sees it jump. A soft sound from the new place and a short message tell the player.
- **Rare and dark**: the room's light is the median frame luminance over the scan. Under 70 Brillo can appear, at most one per round. Spots darker than 70% of the room are "dark" and Dormilón prefers them.
- **Spatial hints**: the hint chirp is panned towards the creature, as in M3, and now also grows louder as the phone turns towards it.
- **Replay** in solo goes straight back to seeking; "Esconder de nuevo" returns to the scan with the spots already found.

## Not in M4b

- Other rare conditions from spec 4.4 (behind plants, late in the day, after many catches) need a species that uses them; the initial set has only Brillo.
